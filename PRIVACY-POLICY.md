# Privacy Policy — Laundry Day Advisor

**Effective date:** 2026-07-28

This Privacy Policy explains what information the Laundry Day Advisor Alexa skill ("the Skill") collects, how it is used, and the choices available to you.

## 1. Who operates this Skill

The Skill is developed and operated by Ricardo Castanho, ("we", "us"). If you have questions about this policy, you can contact us at ricardosoares632@gmail.com.

## 2. Information we collect

To tell you the best day to wash clothes, the Skill needs to know your general location so it can check the weather forecast. Specifically, we collect:

- **The city name you tell the Skill** (for example, "my city is Chicago"), which we convert into approximate geographic coordinates (latitude/longitude) using a third-party geocoding service.
- **Your Alexa-assigned user ID**, provided automatically by Amazon with every request, which we use only to associate your saved city with your account so you don't have to repeat it every time.

We do **not** collect your name, precise device location, address, payment information, or any other personal information beyond what's described above.

## 3. How we use this information

We use the city/coordinates you provide solely to:

- Request a weather forecast (rain chance and wind) for your area.
- Determine and tell you whether today, or which day this week, is best for doing laundry.

We do not use your information for advertising, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to answering your laundry-day questions.

## 4. How this information is stored

Your saved city and its coordinates are stored in a database (Amazon DynamoDB) associated with your Alexa user ID, so the Skill can remember it for future requests. This data is retained until you ask us to delete it or until you disable/uninstall the Skill, at which point Amazon may delete the associated data per Alexa Skills Kit data-retention practices.

## 5. Third-party services

To calculate the best laundry day, the Skill sends the city name or coordinates you provide to [Open-Meteo](https://open-meteo.com/), a third-party weather and geocoding service, solely to retrieve forecast data. Open-Meteo does not receive your Alexa user ID or any other identifying information from us. Please refer to Open-Meteo's own policies for how they handle request data.

## 6. Children's privacy

This Skill is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

## 7. Your choices

- You can update your saved city at any time by telling the Skill a new city.
- You can ask us to delete your saved location by contacting us at ricardosoares632@gmail.com, or by disabling the Skill in the Alexa app, which removes your association with the Skill.

## 8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

## 9. Contact us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or your data, contact us at <ricardosoares632@gmail.com>.
